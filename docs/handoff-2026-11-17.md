## Decisions already made (see docs/superpowers/specs/)
- Default language English; also Spanish and German. Free, no ads, no in-game donation requests.
- No copying of other addons. DBM and Details! are "All Rights Reserved"; Naowh Forever too.
- Quests will be our own addon, a step-by-step guide with original routes and a route
  recorder (design drafted). Not a Questie fork.
- Monetization: none in the addon; any paid services would live outside the game, later.
- Packaging: an addon cannot install others in the game. Own addons ship in our zip; an
  installer/manager for third-party addons fetches from official sources (study done).
